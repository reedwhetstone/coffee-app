# Outline: Why Can't Coffee Remember Its Best Farms?

**Pillar:** market-intelligence
**Target:** about 1,200 words
**Status:** drafted
**Source material:** Reed's concept note and feedback (#blog-post-context-is-everything, 2026-10-02); `notes/research/producer-track-record/`

## Thesis

Reputation is the stored history of quality. Wine built persistent identity first, and its prices then accumulated against that identity. Buffett read a business's durability from long numeric records, and later research showed most of his "full picture" left fingerprints in hard data. Green coffee produces plenty of narrative but throws away the record. Listings rarely carry a durable producer identity, supplier signals are not comparable across suppliers, and the one long public record, Cup of Excellence, shows that quality persists only weakly and is priced only thinly.

## Structure

1. Opening: a curious buyer cannot tell which of three explanations (age, harvest, farm) accounts for a disappointing lot.
2. Buffett read the record: acquisition criteria, See's Candies, Frazzini/Kabiller/Pedersen.
3. Wine stored reputation first: phylloxera fraud led to appellations; Combris et al.; Oczkowski meta-analysis.
4. Coffee writes the story and discards the record: Purveyors catalog numbers, supplier-relative signals, the identity premium against story length, Ethiopia ECX.
5. What a coffee track record looks like: the original Cup of Excellence persistence study.
6. A record has to say who claimed what: credence goods, Kona, Gesha DNA, the eco-label taste bias, value capture.
7. Close: the missing piece is history, and wine kept it by making the name durable.

## Decisions

- Fantasy football analogy dropped as too corny (Reed).
- Buffett and wine are the governing comparisons.
- The disappointing natural stays anonymized; no supplier is named.
- Claims to avoid: that direct trade raises farm-gate prices, that named producers raise consumer willingness to pay, or that wine labels guarantee quality.
- The lot-level Cup of Excellence dataset stays out of the public repo until republishing rights are settled.
